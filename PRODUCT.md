# MediBytes
Register: brand. A clinical voice-to-template platform in development for Indian clinicians and hospital teams, also presented to prospective investors. Turn dictation into structured clinical documents with mandatory human review. Planned languages: English, Hindi, Tamil and mixed-language speech. Planned output: DOCX/PDF/JSON. Planned deployment: cloud or local. Do not claim proven accuracy, regulatory certification, customers, or production availability. Reference: SynthChat Framer, dark and green. Voice: precise, calm, optimistic. This site presents the product, not a functioning clinical service. No real patient data or uploads.
