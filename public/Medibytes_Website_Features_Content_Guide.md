# MediBytes Website Feature Architecture & Content Guide

## Purpose

This document provides the recommended website structure, content
hierarchy, feature positioning, and copy for presenting the **16
MediBytes modules as 5 clear product pillars**.

The goal is to avoid presenting MediBytes like technical documentation.
Instead, the website should communicate a simple message:

> **MediBytes sits on top of the hospital workflow and turns fragmented
> clinical information into structured documentation, clinical
> intelligence, and cleaner revenue workflows.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Recommended Website Structure

The Features / Platform section should be presented in **three layers**.

## Layer 1 --- Five Product Pillars

Display five large, premium visual cards:

1.  **OPD Intelligence**
2.  **IPD Intelligence**
3.  **Clinical Intelligence**
4.  **Document Intelligence**
5.  **Insurance Intelligence**

Each card should contain:

-   A short headline
-   One clear value proposition
-   A minimal product visual or micro-animation
-   An **Explore →** interaction

When the visitor clicks or expands a pillar, the individual capabilities
underneath it should appear.

This keeps the homepage simple while still communicating the depth of
the MediBytes platform.

# 3. OPD Intelligence

## Less typing. More medicine.

**MediBytes helps clinicians convert consultations into structured
clinical documentation while they focus on the patient.**

The platform can capture relevant clinical information from
consultations and assist in generating structured notes that fit into
the hospital's existing workflow.

### Core Capability

#### AI-Assisted OPD Documentation

**Turn clinical conversations and inputs into structured documentation
designed for faster, more consistent outpatient workflows.**

Key capabilities:

-   Structured consultation notes
-   Clinical information capture
-   Documentation assistance within existing workflows

### Recommended Website Visual

Show a consultation workflow:

**Doctor + Patient Conversation → MediBytes Intelligence → Structured
Clinical Note**

The generated note can visually populate fields such as:

-   Chief Complaint
-   History
-   Examination
-   Assessment
-   Plan

### Design Direction

Avoid generic AI robots or brain graphics.

The AI should feel like an **invisible intelligence layer working behind
the clinical workflow**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. IPD Intelligence

## Documentation that follows the patient, not the paperwork.

**MediBytes connects the documentation generated throughout an inpatient
stay --- from admission and rounds to nursing, ICU care, handovers, and
discharge.**

Instead of healthcare teams repeatedly recreating information, MediBytes
helps transform existing clinical inputs into structured documentation
throughout the patient's hospital journey.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.1 Progress Notes

### Turn clinical rounds into structured progress documentation.

MediBytes can assist in capturing changes in the patient's condition,
investigations, treatment decisions, and clinical plans during rounds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.2 Handover Notes

### Make every transition of care clearer.

Automatically structure important patient information for clinical
handovers, helping teams understand what happened, what matters now, and
what needs to happen next.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.3 Nursing Assessment & Care Plans

### Structured nursing documentation without starting from scratch.

Assist nursing teams in converting patient assessments and clinical
information into organised nursing documentation and care plans.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.4 Discharge Summary

### Build the discharge summary throughout the admission.

Instead of reconstructing an entire hospital stay at discharge,
MediBytes can consolidate relevant diagnoses, investigations,
procedures, medications, and clinical events into a structured draft.

This capability should be positioned as a **continuous documentation
workflow**, not simply as an "AI discharge summary."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.5 E-Prescription

### From clinical decisions to structured prescriptions.

Assist clinicians in generating clear, structured electronic
prescriptions from documented treatment plans.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.6 Shift Summary

### Know what changed during the shift.

MediBytes can consolidate clinically relevant events, treatment changes,
and pending actions into concise shift summaries for the incoming team.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.7 ICU Flow Sheet Mapping

### From bedside activity to structured ICU data.

**MediBytes helps map clinical information generated during ICU care
into structured flow-sheet fields, reducing repetitive documentation
across high-intensity environments.**

### Recommended Visual

Show multiple ICU information sources flowing through MediBytes:

**Doctor + Nurse + Monitor + Medication**

↓

**MediBytes**

↓

**Vitals \| Ventilator \| I/O \| Drugs \| Events \| Notes**

This should be one of the more visually sophisticated product
demonstrations on the website.

inconsistencies that may contribute to claim queries, delays, or

documentation and hospital revenue workflows**, rather than as another
administrative module.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.1 Documentation Completeness

MediBytes can review relevant claim documentation and identify
potentially missing information before submission.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.2 Claim Consistency

### One patient. One consistent clinical story.

MediBytes can compare information across clinical documentation,
investigations, procedures, medications, and billing-related records to
surface inconsistencies for review.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7.3 Documentation Assistance

### Find the gap. Fix it earlier.

hospital teams toward the documentation that may need clarification or
completion.

### Supporting Statement

> **Cleaner documentation. Fewer avoidable queries. Faster revenue
> cycles.**

Avoid guaranteeing reductions in claim denials until supported by
validated deployment data.

Do **not** display every capability simultaneously on the homepage.

The interface should progressively reveal detail.

For example:

1.  Visitor sees **IPD Intelligence**

3.  IPD card expands
4.  Progress Notes, Handover, Nursing, Discharge, E-Prescription, Shift
    Summary, and ICU Flow Sheet Mapping appear

This provides depth without overwhelming the visitor.

# 11. Visual Design Language

MediBytes should communicate:

**Clinical Precision + Enterprise Software + Intelligence**

The website should avoid looking like a generic AI SaaS startup.

## Recommended Style

-   Predominantly white interface
-   Strong black typography
-   Generous whitespace
-   Thin neutral borders
-   Minimal shadows
-   Restrained accent colour
-   Structured medical information
-   Product UI visualisations
-   Smooth micro-interactions
-   Clear information hierarchy
-   Premium enterprise appearance

## Avoid

-   Neon gradients everywhere
-   Generic AI brains
-   Robot illustrations
-   Random circuitry
-   Excessive glowing effects
-   Generic stock photographs of doctors
-   Repeated "AI-powered" badges
-   Overloaded dashboards
-   Too many medical icons

The technology should feel **quiet, precise, and embedded into the
workflow**.

# 18. Writing Guidelines for the Website Team

All MediBytes website copy should follow these principles.

## Keep Headlines Short

Good:

> **See what matters first.**

Avoid:

> **Our advanced artificial intelligence system helps doctors identify
> important information.**

## Describe Outcomes Before Technology

Good:

> **Turn hospital paperwork into usable data.**

Then explain document extraction technology.

## Use Clinical Language Carefully

Prefer:

-   "assist"
-   "surface"
-   "identify potential"
-   "for clinician review"
-   "support"
-   "help"
-   "structured draft"

Avoid unsupported claims such as:

-   "eliminates medical errors"
-   "guarantees accuracy"
-   "prevents all medication errors"
-   "eliminates claim denials"
-   "replaces clinical judgement"

## Make AI Secondary

Do not put "AI-powered" into every headline.

The visitor should first understand **what MediBytes does**.

The underlying AI can then explain **how MediBytes does it**.
